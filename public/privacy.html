100% OFFLINE & PRIVACY GUARANTEE

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2, 2026 · Version: 1.0.0

1. Zero Data Collection Architecture

Ember is engineered from the ground up as a 100% offline-first application. We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data, usage logs, health records, or identifiers to remote servers.

  • No Accounts Required: You never need to provide an email, phone number, name, or password.
  • Zero Telemetry & Third-Party Trackers: The application contains no analytics SDKs (no Google Analytics, Facebook SDK, Mixpanel, Firebase, or advertising trackers).
  • No Network Permissions: The Android application binary requests zero network permissions (android.permissions: []).

2. Local Data Storage & Encryption

All your quit statistics, personal reasons, craving timestamps, reflection notes, and daily check-ins reside solely in a local SQLite database on your device file system. Your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly initiate an encrypted backup.

3. Backup & Data Portability

You have complete control over your data:

  • Export: You can export your data at any time into a human-readable, schema-validated JSON file using your device's native share sheet.
  • Import: You can restore a previously exported JSON backup file on any compatible device.
  • Permanent Deletion: You can permanently wipe all personal profile records, craving history, and reflections instantly via the "Delete All Data" feature in Settings.

4. Biometric Protection (App Lock)

If you enable App Lock in Settings, biometric authentication (Face ID, Touch ID, or Android Biometric Prompt) is verified locally by your operating system's secure enclave. Ember never has access to your raw biometric fingerprints or facial geometry.

5. Contact & Inquiries

For questions or verification regarding our offline architecture and privacy commitments, contact our team at: privacy@ember.internal.
